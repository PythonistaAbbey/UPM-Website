# Content Inventory

Summary of content types found, where they live today, and what to do with them. Row-level detail is in `URL_INVENTORY.csv` (122 rows).

| Content type | Where it lives now | Volume seen | Quality notes | Proposed action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Programme names (UG) | spel undergraduate page | ~95 programmes, 16 faculties | Plain list, typos, no detail pages | **Migrate** into structured programme type; verify each with Division of Admission |
| Postgraduate qualifications | spel postgraduate page, sgs | 5 research qualifications; coursework list behind A–Z links | Contradictory counts | **Migrate + reconcile** |
| Admission pathways | spel How to Apply, akademik | 3 Malaysian UG routes, international UG, PG | Inconsistent channel naming | **Consolidate** into pathway pages |
| Institutional facts (history, vision, mission) | spel About Us | ~15 pages | Some last updated 2023 | **Migrate**, owner review |
| Leadership pages | spel About Us > Leadership | 10 pages | Not fetched | **Migrate**, verify names at launch |
| Unit directory | spel entiti | ~110 units | Mostly http links | **Migrate** into organisational unit type |
| News / articles / headlines / activities | Every unit site | Unknown (100+ archives) | Often undated, stale | **Consolidate** into central news with unit tags; archive older items |
| Academic calendars | sgs, akademik | Several | Some only as text | **Migrate** as structured events + PDF |
| Documents (PDF) | pspk, akademik, unit "documentfile" pages | Unknown | Not audited | **Inventory with owners** before migration |
| Operational system links | spel, idec, hep, lib | 25+ | http, ports, no descriptions | **Service directory** entries |
| Tender and job widgets | Every unit template | ~110 copies | Mostly "No quotation" | **Retire** duplicates; one central link |
| Media (gallery, video) | spel and units | Unknown | Rights unknown | **Rights check** before reuse |

Not inventoried: the content of the ~90 unit sites not fetched, all PDFs, and any media files.
